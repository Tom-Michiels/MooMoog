# 17 slide-switch caps

R0.5 omits the external-input switch, leaving 17 caps: 2 orange, 5 blue, 6 white and 4 black. The complete nominal set and separate colour sets are supplied.

Print the three-part fit set first. From left to right in the STL: tight / nominal / loose. Socket sizes are 1.6 × 4.0, 1.8 × 4.1 and 2.0 × 4.2 mm respectively, all 4 mm deep. Exterior is 5 × 5 × 12 mm, with grip grooves and a slightly widened entry. The actual switch actuator thickness remains unverified; do not force a tight cap onto the switch.

The print files put the visible top on the bed and the socket upward. Use the same material/settings for fit trial and final parts. If the nominal fit is unsuitable, duplicate the correct individual STL in the slicer instead of printing the nominal full set.

The proposed panel slot is 5.4 × 10.5 mm. Actual slider travel, printer tolerance and mounting height still need physical confirmation. CAD validity is not a proof of physical fit.
